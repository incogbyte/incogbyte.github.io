About Me

Hi, I’m Rodolfo Tavares, a Pentester and Information Security Consultant with over 8 years of expertise in the cybersecurity field.

  • Throughout my career, I’ve conducted penetration tests on a wide range of assets, including:
    • ATMs (Automated Teller Machines)
    • POS systems (Point-of-Sale terminals)
    • Web applications
    • Mobile applications
    • Infrastructure environments
    • Red Team engagements
    • Code Review / AppSec

My work involves identifying vulnerabilities, assessing risks, and providing actionable recommendations to improve security posture for organizations of all sizes. I’m passionate about offensive security, continuously learning new techniques a.k.a life long learner.
