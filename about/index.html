About

I'm Rodolfo Tavares, also known as incogbyte. I'm a hacker and security researcher with more than 10 years of experience in offensive security. My work covers application security, mobile security, reverse engineering, low-level vulnerabilities, infrastructure security, and exploit development. Over the years, I've found and exploited vulnerabilities across web applications, APIs, iOS, Android, and infrastructure software. You'll find some of that work published here through CVEs and technical write-ups. I'll keep adding new research and revisiting older work that I have not documented yet. I hope you find it useful.
